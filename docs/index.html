Built For Geometry Dash Players

Everything you need to run, protect, and optimize your modded game in one clean desktop companion.

⚡

1-Click 2.2081 Downgrader

Geometry Dash 2.209+ breaks your Geode mods. GeodeSync downloads clean 2.2081 depot files through Steam and locks your installation so Steam won't overwrite your game.

🩹

Entry Point Crash Auto-Repair

Fixes fatal Windows DLL "Entry Point Not Found" errors automatically. Pre-heals known crashing dependencies (like ImagePlus) before launching.

🚀

Kernel CPU Boost & FPS Unlocker

Direct Windows HIGH_PRIORITY_CLASS CPU scheduling for near-zero click latency, plus custom internal FPS unlocked up to 480Hz without paid external trainers.

🎵

NONG Custom Song Manager

Easily swap any level's official audio with custom Not-On-Newgrounds tracks in 1 click, complete with automated backups of the original song files.

🩺

Save Doctor & Auto-Backup

Tests, validates, and recovers corrupted save files with native decryptors. Silently takes automated save snapshots every time GD closes.

🎮

Always-On Discord Presence

Displays your live Geometry Dash stats, current playing state, stars, and demons directly on your Discord profile card with custom 3D artwork.
