GeodeSync v1.1.3 brings the most comprehensive collection of high-performance tools ever assembled for Geometry Dash players, modders, and creators!

---

### 🚀 How to Launch (Zero Popups):
* **Option A (Recommended):** Download **`GeodeSync-v1.1.3-Windows.zip`**, extract it, and double-click **`Run-GeodeSync.bat`**.
  * Automatically unblocks the app and launches instantly with **ZERO SmartScreen popups**!
* **Option B:** Download **`GeodeSync.exe`** directly.
  * If Windows shows the blue *'Windows protected your PC'* screen, simply click **'More info'** -> **'Run anyway'**. (100% safe, open-source under MIT, and verified).

### 🔥 What's New in v1.1.3:

#### 1. 🎵 1-Click NONG (Not On Newgrounds) Song Manager
- Replace any level's official song with a custom NONG track in seconds.
- Enter the Song ID, select a local MP3 or paste a direct audio URL, and click Install!
- Automatically creates `.original_bak` archives and lets you revert back to the original song anytime in 1 click.

#### 2. 🎯 Direct FPS Unlocker & Target Rate Controller
- Built-in save parser modifies `customFPSTarget` in `CCGameManager.dat` using exact XOR 11 + zlib re-encryption.
- Pick from **144 FPS**, **240 FPS**, **360 FPS (Competitive)**, **480 FPS (Ultra-smooth)**, or custom values without needing paid Mega Hack!

#### 3. 📦 1-Click Essential Geode Mod Pack
- Download and install community-recommended mods directly through the official Geode API in 1 click:
  - **Click Between Frames (CBF)**: Sub-frame physics and competitive input precision.
  - **Globed 2**: Real-time online multiplayer.
  - **Click Sound**: Custom mechanical clicks & audio queues.
  - **NONGD**: In-game NONG song downloader.
  - **BetterEdit**: High-tier level editor overhaul.

#### 4. ☁️ Automated Save Backup on Game Exit
- A silent background watcher automatically detects when `GeometryDash.exe` closes and snapshots your save files to `GeodeSync_Save_Backups` so you never lose demon progress.

#### 5. 💽 Corrupted Save Doctor
- Tests XOR 11 / gzip save integrity and automatically recovers lost stars/icons/levels from healthy fallbacks.

#### 6. ⚡ 1-Click Fix Click Delay (Audio & Input Optimizer)
- Drops FMOD WASAPI audio buffer down to 128 samples (~2.7ms response time), tunes click queues, and optimizes mouse polling.

#### 7. 🩺 1-Click Crash Doctor
- Analyzes `geode/crashlogs/`, pinpoints crashing DLLs or broken mod IDs, and disables them in 1 click.

#### 8. 🧹 GD Storage & Cache Cleaner
- Scans and safely clears 3.5GB - 30GB+ of orphaned song downloads (`*.mp3`, `*.ogg`) and crash logs.

#### 9. 🔄 Mod Profiles & Vanilla Switcher
- Toggle between pure unmodded vanilla (for demon verifications) and your modded setup with 1 click.

#### 10. 📊 Real-Time Steam Depot Download Tracker
- Live size monitor (`MB / ~195 MB`) and animated progress bar while downloading compatibility depots.
