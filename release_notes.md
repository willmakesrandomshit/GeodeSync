## ⚡ GeodeSync v1.1.2 • The Power Update

GeodeSync v1.1.2 is a major upgrade packing highly-requested quality-of-life and performance tools for Geometry Dash players and creators!

---

### 🚀 How to Launch (Zero Popups):
* **Option A (Recommended):** Download **`GeodeSync-v1.1.2-Windows.zip`**, extract it, and double-click **`Run-GeodeSync.bat`**.
  * Automatically unblocks the app and launches instantly with **ZERO SmartScreen popups**!
* **Option B:** Download **`GeodeSync.exe`** directly.
  * If Windows shows the blue *'Windows protected your PC'* screen, simply click **'More info'** -> **'Run anyway'**. (100% safe, open-source under MIT, and verified).

---

### 🔥 What's New in v1.1.2:

#### 1. 📊 Real-Time Steam Depot Download Progress
- Added an animated progress bar and live size monitor (`XX.X MB / ~195 MB` & percentage).
- Automatically polls active depot download staging directory so you always know exactly how fast Steam is pulling the files.

#### 2. ⚡ 1-Click "Fix Click Delay" (Input & Audio Optimizer)
- Geometry Dash players often suffer from 20ms–50ms audio latency on Windows 10/11.
- GeodeSync now configures **FMOD low-latency WASAPI buffer (128 samples)**, optimizes click sound queues, and fine-tunes Click Between Frames (CBF) mouse polling settings for instant, ultra-responsive clicks.

#### 3. 🩺 1-Click "Crash Doctor"
- Game crashing on launch after updating or downgrading?
- Crash Doctor automatically analyzes your latest Geode crash logs (`geode/crashlogs/`), identifies the exact offending mod ID or broken DLL, and lets you disable it in one click with automatic backup.

#### 4. 🚀 Official Geode Auto-Installer
- Missing Geode entirely? GeodeSync now queries the official Geode SDK GitHub Releases API, downloads the latest installer directly, and launches it for you in one click.

#### 5. 🔄 Version Archive Selector
- Switch effortlessly between:
  - **GD 2.2081** (Geode Compatible • Recommended)
  - **GD 2.2074** (Previous 2.2 sub-update)
  - **GD 2.113** (Classic 2.1)
  - Or enter any custom Steam depot manifest ID!
