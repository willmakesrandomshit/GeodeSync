# GeodeSync v2.1.0 • GD Live Player Card, Geode Mod Manager & Boosted Game Launcher

GeodeSync v2.1.0 introduces live player stats, an interactive 1-click Geode Mod Manager, and a high-priority boosted game launcher for zero-latency gameplay!

---

### 🌟 What's New in v2.1.0:

* 🏆 **Real-Time GD Player Stats Card:**
  * GeodeSync automatically decrypts and reads your local `CCGameManager.dat` save data.
  * Displays your in-game username (`wilplaysgd`), total stars ⭐, demons beaten 👿, moons 🌙, diamonds 💎, secret + user coins 🪙, and total orbs 🔮 directly on the dashboard!
  * Click **`🔄 Refresh`** anytime to sync immediately.
  * Automatically refreshes your stats whenever Geometry Dash closes and snapshots your save files.

* 🎮 **Enhanced Discord Rich Presence:**
  * Displays your live stats right on your Discord activity profile card:
    * In-game: `Playing Geometry Dash` / `416 ⭐ · 16 👿 · Geode v5.11.2 🛡️`
    * Idle / Companion: `Geometry Dash Companion` / `wilplaysgd (416 ⭐ · 16 👿)`
  * Friends on Discord can now see your stars and demon count in real time!

* 🧩 **Interactive Geode Mod Manager:**
  * Replaces the static mods folder opener with an interactive modal window.
  * Real-time search filter by mod title, author, or mod ID.
  * **1-Click Enable / Disable** toggles for every installed mod (`.geode` ↔ `.geode.disabled`) without losing your mod configurations.
  * Bulk actions: **`✅ Enable All`** and **`⛔ Disable All`** to troubleshoot lag or corrupted mods instantly.
  * Quick-launch button to open the physical mods directory in File Explorer.

* 🚀 **High-Priority "Boosted" Game Launcher:**
  * Added **`▶ Launch GD (Boosted)`** right in the primary tools drawer.
  * Launches `GeometryDash.exe` with Windows kernel High CPU Priority (`HIGH_PRIORITY_CLASS`), ensuring minimum frame drops, no audio crackling, and ultra-low input latency for Click Between Frames (CBF) and Demon grinding.
